    • CriticalClaims a “100% WIN RATE”. No real trader has one. This claim alone is disqualifying — and would be illegal in a regulated firm's marketing.
    • Critical“Proof” is forwarded MT4 history screenshots, not a verified track record. Screenshots are trivially faked: demo accounts, cherry-picked legs of hedged positions, editing tools. Verified = live Myfxbook / FX Blue link.

    • CriticalSteers to an offshore broker with no UK protection. Channels typically earn introducing-broker kickbacks per lot you trade — they get paid whether you win or lose, more if you overtrade.
